## 1. International CEO from Tel Aviv (Finout)

**Persona fit:** CEO/Founder, CFO, Global Mobility, VC Talent<br>**Role/situation:** International executive, no US credit or SSN

**The challenge:**<br>CEO relocating from Tel Aviv to NYC. No US credit history, no SSN, strict apartment criteria, and no time for a drawn-out search. Standard landlord requirements (40× annual income, 700+ US credit) would have led to immediate denial.

**What we did:**

* Pre-qualified buildings near Columbus Circle that met his strict criteria
* Coordinated guarantor and documentation package up front
* Secured lease approval in 48 hours despite no US credit
* Delivered a permanent furnished home ready on his arrival

**Outcome:** Lease approved, keys in hand, zero delay to start date.

## 2. West Coast Founder Relocating While Running Company

**Persona fit:** CEO/Founder<br>**Role/situation:** Domestic executive, severely time-constrained

**The challenge:**<br>Founder relocating to NYC while still actively running his company on the West Coast. Couldn't afford multiple trips or extended in-person touring. Needed a full-service approach with minimal time commitment.

**What we did:**

* Consolidated all showings into one day of touring
* Handled all documentation, approvals, and logistics remotely
* Founder stayed focused on running the business throughout the process

**Outcome:** Permanent housing secured in one day of in-person touring. No lost execution cycles.

## 5. Married Engineering Couple from India

**Persona fit:** People Ops, CFO, Global Mobility<br>**Role/situation:** International, dual-hire, family situation

**The challenge:**<br>Two technical hires — married couple — relocating from India. Company budget allowed only 30 days of temp housing. Risk of extension fees and delayed productivity for both engineers if permanent housing wasn't found fast.

**What we did:**

* Coordinated tours immediately on landing
* Secured permanent housing within one week
* Avoided 30-day temp housing extensions entirely

**Outcome:** Permanent housing in one week. No temp housing extension fees. Both engineers Day One productive.

## 6. Salesperson from Texas with 65-lb Dog

**Persona fit:** Recruiters (GTM/Sales), People Ops<br>**Role/situation:** Domestic, GTM/Sales, pet constraint

**The challenge:**<br>Sales hire relocating from Texas to NYC with a 65-lb dog. Most NYC landlords have weight restrictions or pet bans. Risk of disqualification from most listings.

**What we did:**

* Targeted only verified pet-friendly landlords
* Pre-confirmed pet policies before any tours
* Prioritized buildings with park access
* Secured remote approval

**Outcome:** Compliant apartment secured. No delayed start. Pet-friendly building with park access.
